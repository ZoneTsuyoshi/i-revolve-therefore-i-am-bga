I Revolve, Therefore I Am

— Once, you were someone —

This is the Psycho Cloister.

Your inner world exists as a labyrinth, and that labyrinth is the place that defines you. Several personalities live in it, each making its rounds from room to room.

When thought reaches its peak, an ego is established and can escape this cycle of rebirth. In this space, a few “interpretations” are accepted as establishing an ego:

- Assembling the countless scattered fragments of the self.

- Recalling in an instant, and discovering what the self truly is.

- Reaching the deepest layer of the subconscious and unifying the whole mind.

But remember: in this world, only one ego can be established. To establish one ego is to lose every other.

Will you remain here? Or will you cross over beyond perception? This is your one and only chance to prove that you are you.
