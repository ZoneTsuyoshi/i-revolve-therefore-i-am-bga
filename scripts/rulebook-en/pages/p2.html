Sort the floor cards by level and lay them out as the cloister. For your first game, place them in ID order starting from the top left. Once you know the game, shuffle each level separately, then place the level 2 floor cards in the four corners and the level 1 floor cards in the other 8 spaces.
Sort the Memory cards by level and shuffle each level to form its deck. With 2/3 players, remove 52/26 cards from the level 1 Memory deck from the game. From here on, “the Memory deck” by itself means the level 1 Memory deck.
Draw 8 cards face down from the Memory deck and place 1 on each level 1 floor card. In the same way, draw 4 cards face down from the level 2 Memory deck and place 1 on each level 2 floor card.
Draw 6 cards from the Memory deck and place them face up as the open cards.
Shuffle the Mind Track cards of each tier separately, then lay out 2/1/2/1/1 cards face up for tiers 1/2/3/4/5.
Place a Psychic Light token on each Mind Track card of tiers 1/2/3/4.
Shuffle the Enhanced Ego cards, take as many as there are players, and place them as a face-down pile beside the tier 3 Mind Track cards.
Shuffle the Goal cards and lay out 3/4/5 of them face up for 2/3/4 players.
Place the tokens matching each floor card’s Tour Bonus in piles near the level 2 rooms in the four corners.
