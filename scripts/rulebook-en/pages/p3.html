Each player chooses a color and takes the meeple and cube of that color.
Place each player’s cube below the tier 1 Mind Track cards.
The player who established their ego earliest is the start player, and turn order goes clockwise from there. Give each player 1 Psychic Light and 1 Omni-Move Key token. Then give each player the following, according to turn order:
1st: 2 Memory cards
2nd: 3 Memory cards, 1 Psychoanalysis token
3rd: 4 Memory cards, 1 Construction token
4th: 5 Memory cards, 1 Psychic Light, 1 Construction token
Shuffle the Initial Ego cards and take as many as there are players plus 1. In reverse turn order, each player secretly chooses 1 and passes the rest to the next player. When all players have chosen, everyone reveals their Initial Ego card.
In reverse turn order, each player places their meeple in a corner room that does not have a meeple yet.
Each player reaching tier 3 secretly looks at the fronts and takes 1 card.
Floor action: the action you take when you stop here. “/” means the left or the right action.
Tour Bonus: gained when you go around this corner.
