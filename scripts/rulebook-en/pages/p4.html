3. How to Play

The start player takes the first turn. After that, players take turns in clockwise order.

On your turn, carry out the 5 phases on the right in order.

See the rules that follow for the details of each phase.

Move phase: Move your meeple
Recall phase: Trigger the recall effect
Floor Action phase: Take the floor action
Construction phase: Construct (≈ build your engine)
Cleanup phase: Prepare for the next turn
Move Phase: Move Your Meeple
In this phase, you must move your meeple clockwise to another room. When you move, follow these rules:
Important: Effects of Memory cards and Ego cards marked “Turn start” must be resolved before the Move phase.
You must move at least 1 room.
To enter a room, you must pay the move cost shown on the back of its Memory card.
If the room after your current room is a level 2 room, you may skip that level 2 room and go on to the room after it. A skipped room does not count as a room you “entered.”
When you enter or skip a level 2 room, gain the Tour Bonus shown on its floor card.
Pay move costs with any combination of the 4 methods a–d.
a) Use constructed cards
Turn a constructed card sideways (used) to use its keys. You cannot split the keys of a card with several keys across separate moves. However, when you move through consecutive rooms, they count as used at the same time.
b) Discard from your hand
Discard a Memory card from your hand to use the keys shown on it. The detailed rules are the same as a).
c) Use Psychic Light
The Xth time you pay Psychic Light as a move cost during this turn’s move, pay X Psychic Light to use it as 1 key of any type.
d) Use an Omni-Move Key
Flip an Omni-Move Key token to its used side to pay for 1 key of any type.
