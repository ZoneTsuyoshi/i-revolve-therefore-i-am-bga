Recall Phase: Trigger the Recall Effect

In this phase, resolve the recall effect shown on the Memory card of the room you are in.

Turn the Memory card of your room face up.
Every player in that room triggers the card’s recall effect. However, if the recall effect replaces the floor action, only the turn player can trigger it. When the order matters between several players (for example, “take an open card”), resolve it clockwise starting with the turn player.
Take that room’s Memory card.
Example: Maria turns the Memory card of the level 2 room just reached face up. The recall effect of the revealed card, “Labyrinth of Knowledge,” is “Instead of the floor action, you may take a Psychoanalysis action with cost -1.” Maria decides to take the Psychoanalysis action instead of the floor action. Bob is in the same room and would normally trigger the recall effect too, but because this recall effect replaces the floor action, Bob cannot trigger it.
Floor Action Phase: Take the Floor Action
In this phase, take the floor action shown on the floor card of the room you are in. However, if a recall effect lets you replace the floor action, you may take the replacement action instead.
Example: The floor action of HESTIA, the room Maria reached, is “gain 1 Psychic Light token, take 1 open card, and draw 1 card from the deck.” However, Maria applies the recall effect and performs Psychoanalysis with cost -1 instead of the floor action.
Construction Phase: Construct (≈ Build Your Engine)
In this phase, you may take a Construction action. Construction actions are explained in “4. Actions.”
Cleanup Phase: Prepare for the Next Turn
In this phase, do a–d to prepare for the next player’s turn.
a) Refresh constructed cards
Turn your sideways constructed cards back upright.
b) Refresh Omni-Move Keys
Flip your used Omni-Move Key tokens back face up.
c) Refill rooms
On each floor card of the cloister with no Memory card, place 1 Memory card of the matching level face down.
d) Refill the open cards
If there are fewer than 6 open cards, add Memory cards face up until there are 6.
