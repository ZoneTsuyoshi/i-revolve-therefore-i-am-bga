4. Actions
The main actions of this game are Construction and Psychoanalysis. Actions happen in the Floor Action phase, in the Construction phase, or as free actions.
Construction Action: Play a Memory Card from Your Hand to Your Constructed Area

With this action, you pay Construction energy to construct a Memory card from your hand.

You can take this action through 1 the Construction phase, 2 a floor action, or 3 paying a Construction token.

Pay Construction energy with any combination of the 2 methods a and b.

Paying Construction tokens:
You can use Construction tokens before or after the Construction phase; each gives you an additional action. You may use as many as you hold, and there is no limit to how many you can hold. If the tokens run out, use a suitable substitute.
a) Discard from your hand
Each discarded Memory card pays Construction energy equal to its level.
Note: A discarded level 2 Memory card counts as 2 energy.
b) Use Psychic Light
The Xth time you pay Psychic Light as energy in this Construction action, pay X Psychic Light to use it as 1 energy. For example, 2/3 energy costs 3/6 Psychic Light.
Important: The Psychic Light needed for “Use Psychic Light” resets for each action and phase. For example, even if you paid Psychic Light in an action from a Construction token, in the action of your Construction phase 1/3/6 Psychic Light still pays 1/2/3 energy.

Free Actions
Paying Construction / Psychoanalysis tokens
Before or after the Construction phase, you may take additional actions with Construction tokens and Psychoanalysis tokens. You may use more than one token.
Claiming a Goal card
Up to once per turn, you may claim 1 Goal card whose condition you meet. Goal cards give you additional Construction Score.
