Psychoanalysis Action: Advance on the Mind Track

With this action, you pay the Psychoanalysis cost to move up 1 tier on the Mind Track.

You can take this action through 1 a floor action or 2 paying a Psychoanalysis token.

Pay the Psychoanalysis cost with any combination of the 3 methods a–c.

Paying Psychoanalysis tokens:
You can use Psychoanalysis tokens before or after the Construction phase; each gives you an additional action. You may use as many as you hold, and there is no limit to how many you can hold. If the tokens run out, use a suitable substitute.
a) Use constructed cards
Turn a constructed card sideways (used) to use its keys. A card with several keys can pay several costs at once, but you cannot split its keys with a move or another action.
b) Discard from your hand
Discard a Memory card from your hand to use the keys shown on it. The detailed rules are the same as a).
c) Use Psychic Light
The Xth time you pay Psychic Light as a cost in this Psychoanalysis action, pay X Psychic Light to use it as 1 key of any type. For example, 2/3 keys cost 3/6 Psychic Light.
Important: The Psychic Light needed for “Use Psychic Light” resets for each action and phase.

As its BONUS, Toshiki secretly looks at the fronts of the Enhanced Ego cards and takes “Capricious.” Thanks to “Capricious,” Toshiki gains every turn, starting from Toshiki’s next turn.
Psychoanalysis cost: The cost required to advance to this card.
Psychoanalysis bonus: The immediate bonus you gain when you advance to this card.
5. Game End and Object of the Game
There are 3 victory conditions in this game. A player who meets any one of them wins immediately. In addition, if a deck of Memory cards runs out, all players lose (there is no winner).
Fleeting Recollection
Enter every room in a single turn. The room you are in at the start of your turn does not count as entered, so you must go all the way around and enter it.
Gathered Fragments
Reach a total Construction Score of 20 or more. You get score from Memory cards and Goal cards.
Pinnacle of the Mind
Reach tier 5 of the Mind Track.
